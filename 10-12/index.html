The Quokka

The quokka is a small marsupial found in Western Australia. It is known for its friendly appearance and is often called the "happiest animal on Earth." Quokkas are nocturnal and primarily herbivorous, feeding on leaves, stems, and bark.


Where They Live

Quokkas are native to a few small islands off the coast of Western Australia, including Rottnest Island and Bald Island. They can also be found in small populations on the mainland, particularly in protected areas. These marsupials prefer habitats with dense vegetation that provides cover and food sources.
